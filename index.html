    What does this age mean?

    It is the age of a same-sex reference person with equal model-predicted five-year mortality risk. It is not remaining life expectancy or a direct measurement of biological lung aging.

    Research use

    Developed in NHANES 2007–2012. Historical NHANES III evaluation showed underprediction of mortality. The model is not established for clinical decisions or populations outside its development setting.

    The reference covers ages 50–79. Risks outside that reference receive a direction flag, not a clipped or extrapolated numerical age.

    Input definitions

    Diagnoses refer to baseline history. Chronic lung disease means emphysema or chronic bronchitis. Smoking duration is elapsed years from initiation to current age or cessation, not pack-years. In the default mode, measured FEV₁ and FVC are automatically standardized using age, sex and height. Advanced mode accepts existing GLI Global z scores.
